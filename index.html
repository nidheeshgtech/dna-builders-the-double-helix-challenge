How to play

  • Drag a block from the tray onto the glowing space, or just click a block to send it there.
  • The glowing space is on your side of the ladder: left for the first builder, right for the second.
  • Press Check answer. Right pair: green glow and points. Wrong pair: Ms. Rivera gives you a hint and you try again.
  • A pairs with T. G pairs with C. U is only found in RNA, so it never goes in DNA.
  • First try = 10 points, second try = 6, after that = 3. Asking the teacher for help = 3.
  • Keyboard: press A T G C U to place a block and Enter to check.
  • Drag the empty classroom to look around. Scroll or pinch to zoom.

Start over?

The ladder will be cleared and the score goes back to zero.
